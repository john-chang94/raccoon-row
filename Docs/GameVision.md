# Raccoon Row game vision

A portrait cozy idle game about building a personal raccoon settlement and watching its residents live there. The player changes the home; the crew makes that change feel useful and alive. The two pillars carry equal weight: autonomous raccoon life and meaningful player-directed settlement growth.

Clean work should preserve the canonical raccoon appearance and the approved flat cozy direction, not the legacy project structure. The courtyard stays trail-free and uncluttered. Progress should feel good on a visit without demanding constant attention.

The source of truth is the [finalized November 18 design brief](References/Dumpster%20Dynasty%20Game%20Vision%20and%20Vertical%20Slice.docx), originally titled **Dumpster Dynasty Game Vision and Vertical Slice.docx**. Use **Raccoon Row** in new project work. Read the brief together with the later corner scavenging HUD and preset zone loot decisions. Prototype tuning and open choices are not permanent design commitments.
